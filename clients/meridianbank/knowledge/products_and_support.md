# Meridian Bank — Products, Cards & Loans

## Savings Accounts
- Meridian Savings: zero-balance option for salary accounts; regular savings needs a monthly average balance of Rs 5,000
- Digital savings account can be opened online with PAN and Aadhaar in about 10 minutes
- Interest is credited quarterly; current published savings rates are on the website — final rate is confirmed by the branch

## Current Accounts
- For businesses and professionals; cheque book, net banking, and overdraft options
- Minimum quarterly balance varies by plan — the team confirms plan details on request

## Cards
- Debit cards: free with savings accounts, contactless, daily withdrawal limit set per card type
- Credit cards: travel, cashback, and secured (against FD) variants
- How to activate a card: use it at any ATM with your PIN, or activate via the mobile app
- To block a lost or stolen card immediately: use the app's Block Card option or call the 24x7 helpline. The bot always escalates lost/stolen/fraud cases to a human.

## Loans
- Home loans: up to 80% of property value; tenures up to 30 years; processing fee applies
- Personal loans: for salaried and self-employed customers; tenures up to 5 years
- Vehicle loans: up to 90% on-road funding for new vehicles
- Education loans: collateral-free up to a published limit for premier institutes

## Eligibility
- Loan eligibility depends on income, credit score, existing obligations, and the product
- The bot NEVER promises approval — eligibility means "you can apply", not "you are approved"
- Check eligibility: share name, phone, and product interest and the team calls back with the published criteria
- Pre-approved offers (if any) are visible ONLY in the mobile app — the bot cannot see or confirm them

## KYC & Documents
- KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, and a recent photograph; re-KYC every 2-10 years depending on account type
- Update KYC at any branch or via the app's video-KYC option

## Branch & Support
- Branch and ATM locator is in the app and website; share your area and the team sends the nearest branch
- Phone banking: 1800 123 4567 (24x7 for card blocking and fraud)
- Business hours: Mon-Fri, 9:00-18:00 IST; branch banking varies by branch
- Grievance: complaints are acknowledged in 24 hours and resolved within RBI-mandated timelines
- The bot NEVER asks for OTP, PIN, passwords, or full card numbers — if anyone does, it is not the bank
