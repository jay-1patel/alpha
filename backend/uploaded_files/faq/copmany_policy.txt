Q: Who is the company behind TrooGood?
A: TrooGood is owned and operated by Mformillet Foods Private Limited, a registered Indian food company that manufactures and sells millet-based chikki and healthy snacks.

Q: What is TrooGood's registered company name?
A: Our registered company name is Mformillet Foods Private Limited.

Q: What is TrooGood's mission?
A: Our mission is to make traditional Indian chikki healthier and more accessible by using millets, clean ingredients, and honest labelling, without artificial preservatives.

Q: Is TrooGood FSSAI licensed?
A: Yes. All TrooGood products are manufactured and packed under a valid FSSAI licence, and the licence number is printed on every product pack.

Q: Where are TrooGood products manufactured?
A: All TrooGood chikki is manufactured at our own FSSAI-licensed manufacturing facility in India under company-controlled quality supervision.

Q: Does TrooGood follow food safety standards?
A: Yes. Our facility follows FSSAI food safety requirements, GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices), and documented hygiene, sanitation, and batch-traceability procedures.

Q: Does TrooGood conduct quality testing?
A: Yes. Every production batch is checked for moisture, texture, weight, sealing integrity, and sensory quality before dispatch, and records are maintained for each batch.

Q: Is every TrooGood pack traceable to a batch?
A: Yes. Every pack carries a batch number, manufacturing date, and best-before date that link it to our internal production and quality records.

Q: Are TrooGood products vegetarian?

Q: Does TrooGood use artificial preservatives?

Q: Does TrooGood use palm oil?
