Q: Can I return a TrooGood Chikki product after it’s shipped?
A: No — since our chikkis are food items, we don’t accept returns once shipped. Exceptions: if the product is damaged, short, wrong, or expired on arrival. Report within 48 hours of delivery for a free replacement or refund.

Q: How do I report a damaged, missing, or wrong item?
A: Email support@troogood.com within 48 hours with your order ID, clear photos of the outer box with the AWB label, inner packaging, and the affected product showing batch/expiry. We’ll resolve it within 24–48 business hours.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, if it hasn’t moved to Processing/Packed. You can cancel within 30 minutes of placing the order or any time before 2:00 PM IST the same business day, whichever is earlier.

Q: What counts as “processed” for cancellations?
A: Status “Processing,” “Packed,” or “Dispatched” means the order is being prepared or has left our warehouse and can’t be cancelled.

Q: How do I request a cancellation?
A: Go to My Orders on troogood.com and click Cancel, or email support@troogood.com with your order ID. You can also call +91-98765-43210 (Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM–6:30 PM IST).

Q: Can I cancel a Cash on Delivery (COD) order?
A: Yes, before dispatch. Please avoid refusing delivery at the doorstep. Repeated COD refusals may lead to COD being disabled for your account.

Q: How long does a refund take after a cancellation?
A: We issue the refund within 2 business days of approving your cancellation. UPI/Wallets typically reflect in 1–3 working days; Cards/NetBanking in 3–7 working days.

Q: How are COD refunds processed?
A: For COD refunds, we’ll send you a secure link to submit your UPI ID or bank details (Account Name/Number and IFSC). Once received, we process the refund in 1–2 business days; credit shows in 1–3 working days. We will never ask for OTPs or passwords.

Q: What if I don’t receive my refund in the stated time?
A: First check with your bank. If it still hasn’t arrived, email support@troogood.com with your order ID and last four digits of the payment method. We’ll share the refund ARN/reference and coordinate with the gateway.

Q: Are large orders (above ₹10,000) handled differently for refunds?
A: Yes. Cancellations after packing but before dispatch are refunded minus a 1.5% payment gateway reversal fee and a ₹99 packing fee. After dispatch, the order can’t be cancelled; refund is processed once the parcel returns to us, minus two-way shipping.

Q: Do you offer replacements instead of refunds?
A: Yes. For damage/shortage/wrong/expired items reported within 48 hours, we can reship replacements at no extra cost, or you can choose a refund.

Q: Are shipping charges refunded?
A: If the issue is on us (damaged, wrong, expired, or courier-lost), we refund the full amount including shipping. For voluntary cancellations before dispatch, shipping (if paid) is refunded. For RTO due to customer reasons (wrong address, unreachable, refused delivery), shipping isn’t refunded and two-way courier charges are deducted.

Q: What happens if I refuse the delivery or the courier can’t reach me?
A: When the parcel returns (RTO), we refund the product value minus two-way shipping: Standard ₹98 or Express ₹178 per order, plus any COD fee if applicable.

Q: Can I return unopened packs for a refund if I change my mind?
A: No. Due to food safety, we don’t accept “change of mind” returns once shipped.

Q: The taste/texture isn’t what I expected. Can I return it?
A: Taste preference isn’t a returnable reason. However, if you believe there’s a quality issue, email photos and details within 48 hours and we’ll investigate.

Q: The product delivered is close to expiry. What can I do?
A: We guarantee at least 75 days of shelf life on dispatch for standard packs. If you received less than this, contact us within 48 hours for a replacement or refund.

Q: I received fewer items than I ordered. What now?
A: Report within 48 hours with photos of the box, inner packaging, and invoice. We’ll ship the missing items or refund the difference within 24–48 business hours.

Q: I received the wrong flavor/variant. What now?
A: Email us within 48 hours with photos. We’ll dispatch the correct item at no cost or process a refund for the item value.

Q: My order shows “Delivered” but I didn’t receive it. Will I get a refund?
A: Report within 48 hours. We’ll pull courier POD/OTP logs and GPS scans (takes 2–4 business days). If mis-delivered or lost, we’ll reship or refund in full.

Q: Can I exchange a product?
A: Exchanges aren’t possible once shipped. If there’s an issue with what you received, we’ll replace or refund per our exception policy.

Q: Do I need to ship the damaged/wrong product back?
A: No. For food safety, we don’t ask for returns. Please keep the product and packaging for 48 hours in case we need additional photos.

Q: Can I cancel part of my order (one item out of many)?
A: Yes, before processing. After dispatch, partial cancellations aren’t possible. For delivered orders with an issue, we’ll resolve item-wise with a replacement/refund.

Q: What happens to coupon discounts during refunds?
A: If your refund makes the order ineligible for a coupon/free shipping threshold, the discount is adjusted on a pro‑rata basis. Used coupon codes aren’t reissued.

Q: I used a gift card/store credit. How is the refund handled?
A: Gift card and store credit amounts are returned to the same wallet. Any remainder paid by card/UPI goes back to the original method.

Q: Do you offer store credit instead of a refund?
A: Yes. Choose TrooGood Wallet for an instant credit within 24 hours and get a 2% bonus (max ₹50) on the refunded amount. Wallet credit is valid for 12 months.

Q: Can I change items in my order instead of cancelling?
A: If the order isn’t processed yet, cancel and place a fresh order. We can’t modify items or quantities after checkout.

Q: Are custom gift boxes or printed gift notes refundable?
A: Custom hampers and printed notes are non‑cancellable once “Packed.” If there’s damage/shortage, we’ll replace the affected contents.

Q: What about pre‑orders?
A: You may cancel up to 48 hours before the stated dispatch date for a full refund. Within 48 hours of dispatch, cancellation isn’t possible.

Q: Do you refund taxes and COD fees?
A: On eligible refunds due to our error or cancellation before dispatch, taxes are fully refunded. COD fees are refunded only if we cancel the order or it’s our courier’s fault.

Q: Payment failed but money was debited. What should I do?
A: Most failed payments auto‑reverse within 5–7 working days. If not, email support@troogood.com with your order attempt time and the bank reference/UTR; we’ll coordinate with our gateway (Razorpay).

Q: I was charged twice for the same order. Can I get a refund?
A: Yes. Share both transaction references; we’ll void the duplicate or refund within 2 business days.

Q: Who do I contact for payment‑related issues?
A: Card/UPI details are processed securely by Razorpay. For bank declines or charge disputes, contact your bank or Razorpay support. You can always write to support@troogood.com and we’ll assist.

Q: How do I escalate a refunds issue?
A: If you haven’t heard back within 48 business hours, email escalate@troogood.com with “Refund Escalation” and your order ID. Our team lead will respond within 1 business day.

Q: Will I get a GST credit note for a partial refund?
A: Yes. For business customers, we issue a GST credit note to the original GSTIN on the invoice and email it within 3–5 business days.

Q: What are your customer support hours?
A: Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–6:30 PM IST (closed on public holidays in Maharashtra). Email: support@troogood.com | Phone/WhatsApp: +91-98765-43210.

Q: In which currency are refunds processed?
A: All refunds are processed in INR to the original payment method or to TrooGood Wallet (if you choose store credit).

Q: Are freebies or promotional items replaced?
A: Freebies are not replaced independently. If your main order has an eligible issue, we’ll replace/refund the affected paid items; freebies are excluded from replacement.
