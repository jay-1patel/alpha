Q: Do you ship outside India?
A: No — we currently ship only within India. Orders placed with an international shipping address are automatically cancelled and refunded.

Q: How long does it take for an order to be processed?
A: Orders placed Monday–Saturday before 2:00 PM IST are packed and dispatched the same day; orders placed after 2:00 PM are dispatched the next business day. We do not dispatch on Sundays or on Maharashtra public holidays.

Q: Where do you ship from?
A: All orders ship from our Fulfilment Centre at Gala 23, Building C, OM Logistics Park, Anjurphata, Bhiwandi, Thane, Maharashtra 421302.

Q: What are the shipping options and costs?
A: Standard Shipping (Surface/Air blend): 3–6 business days to most pincodes — free on orders ₹499 and above; ₹49 on orders below ₹499. Express Shipping (Air): 1–3 business days to metro cities and 2–5 days to others — ₹89 per order. Same/Next-Day Local (Mumbai MMR & Pune only): Next-business-day delivery for orders placed by 11:00 AM — ₹129.

Q: Are there any weight-based shipping charges?
A: Yes. The above rates cover parcels up to 5 kg. For every additional 1 kg (or part), add ₹10 for Standard and ₹20 for Express. Orders above 10 kg ship in multiple boxes at no extra handling fee.

Q: Which courier partners do you use?
A: We ship via Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, and Ecom Express. The partner is auto-selected based on your pincode and service speed.

Q: What are the delivery timelines by location?
A: Metros (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Kolkata): Standard 2–4 business days, Express 1–2. Tier 2/3 cities: Standard 4–7, Express 2–4. Remote/limited-service pincodes: Standard 6–10, Express 3–6.

Q: Do you deliver to my pincode?
A: We service 27,800+ pincodes. Check serviceability on the product page or at checkout. We do not deliver to APO/Defence PO, PO Boxes, or select islands (e.g., Lakshadweep, A&N remote outposts).

Q: Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
A: Yes, COD is available for orders between ₹299 and ₹1,999 on eligible pincodes, with a flat COD fee of ₹30. COD is not available for pre-orders, corporate/wholesale, or custom gift boxes.

Q: What is the order cut-off time for same-day dispatch?
A: 2:00 PM IST Monday–Saturday for regular orders. For MMR/Pune Next-Day Local, the cut-off is 11:00 AM IST.

Q: Do you ship on weekends and holidays?
A: We dispatch Monday–Saturday. Courier partners may deliver on Sundays in many metros, but this varies by pincode. No dispatches on public holidays observed in Maharashtra.

Q: Can I schedule delivery for a specific date or time?
A: Exact time-slot scheduling isn’t supported. You can add a preferred delivery note at checkout; we’ll pass it to the courier, but it’s not guaranteed.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Yes, within 30 minutes of placing the order or until the order is dispatched (whichever is earlier). Email support@troogood.com with your order ID. After dispatch, address changes aren’t possible.

Q: How do I track my shipment?
A: You’ll receive an email/SMS with the AWB number and a tracking link once dispatched. You can also track at https://troogood.com/track using your order ID or AWB.

Q: What happens if I am not available at the time of delivery?
A: The courier will attempt delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the parcel will be returned to us (RTO). Re-shipping can be arranged with a ₹49 re-ship fee (plus any COD fee if applicable).

Q: My order is delayed. What should I do?
A: If your tracking shows no movement for 72 hours or your order exceeds the maximum ETA, email support@troogood.com with your order ID. We’ll raise a priority ticket with the courier and keep you updated.

Q: What if my order shows “Delivered” but I didn’t receive it?
A: Report it within 48 hours to support@troogood.com. We’ll request proof of delivery (POD), GPS/OTP logs, and initiate an investigation. Resolution typically takes 2–4 business days.

Q: Do you ship to PO Boxes or Army Post Offices (APO/DP0)?
A: No. Please provide a complete residential or commercial street address with landmark and active phone number.

Q: Is an OTP or signature required on delivery?
A: For orders above ₹1,500 and all Express shipments, OTP delivery is enabled by default. For Standard shipments, OTP may apply based on courier policy in your area.

Q: Will my chikki melt or spoil during transit?
A: Our chikki is shelf-stable. We use moisture-barrier pouches and double-corrugated boxes. During high heat/monsoon, we add extra cushioning and desiccants. No cold packs are required.

Q: How is the product packed for shipping?
A: Each pack is nitrogen-flushed and sealed, then placed in a crush-proof corrugated box with corner guards and FRAGILE labels to minimize breakage.

Q: Can you leave the package with security or at the doorstep?
A: Yes, if you authorize it in your order notes or via the courier’s delivery SMS. Please note that doorstep/guard deliveries are at the customer’s risk once marked delivered.

Q: Do you split shipments?
A: Yes. If an item is back-ordered or ships from a different zone, we may send multiple parcels at no extra shipping charge. You’ll receive separate tracking for each.

Q: How do you handle bulk/corporate orders?
A: Orders above 50 units may need 1–2 extra business days to dispatch. Palletized shipping or fixed-date deliveries can be arranged on request; email support@troogood.com for a quote.

Q: What if my chikki arrives damaged or short in quantity?
A: Upload the photos of damaged product/chikki in https://troogood.com/damaged_order or Email photos of the outer box, inner packaging, and product labels within 48 hours of delivery to support@troogood.com. We’ll ship a free replacement or issue a refund within 24–48 hours after verification.

Q: Are there any locations you do not ship to?
A: We currently do not ship to: Lakshadweep (all pincodes), A&N remote islands (7442xx), and certain border/remote pincodes flagged as “out of delivery area” by our couriers. Serviceability can change—check at checkout.

Q: Do you charge a “remote area” surcharge?
A: No extra fee, but remote pincodes may add 1–3 business days to the delivery timeline.

Q: What happens if my parcel is lost in transit?
A: If a shipment is confirmed lost by the courier, we’ll dispatch a replacement at no cost or offer a full refund—your choice.

Q: Can I refuse delivery?
A: Yes, but once the parcel returns to us (RTO), we’ll process a refund minus actual outward shipping and COD fee (if any). Re-shipping can be arranged on request.

Q: Do you include an invoice in the package?
A: Yes, a GST invoice is included. For gifts, we can email the invoice only—add a note at checkout.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping and messages?
A: Yes. Add gift wrap and a custom note for ₹25 at checkout. This does not affect shipping timelines.

Q: Will pre-orders delay my entire shipment?
A: By default, your order ships together on the pre-order item’s release date (shown on the product page). You can request split shipping; Express charges may apply to the second parcel.

Q: What are your delivery hours?
A: Couriers generally deliver between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM local time. Exact timing depends on the delivery route for your pincode.

Q: Can I change the shipping method after ordering (e.g., Standard to Express)?
A: Before dispatch, yes—email support@troogood.com. We’ll share a payment link for the difference. After dispatch, the method cannot be changed.

Q: Do high order volumes affect shipping?
A: During peak weeks (Diwali, Christmas/New Year), dispatch may take an extra 24 hours. We’ll notify you by email if your order is affected.
