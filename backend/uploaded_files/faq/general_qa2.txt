Q: How do you ensure the peanuts don't have harmful aflatoxins?
A: Aflatoxin control is a strict part of our quality policy. We source our peanuts directly from Saurashtra and test every raw batch for aflatoxin levels using ELISA testing in our in-house lab. The maximum permissible limit in our facility is strictly set below 10 ppb (parts per billion). Batches failing this test are rejected and destroyed.
