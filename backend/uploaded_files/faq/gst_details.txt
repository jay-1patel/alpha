Q: Will I get a GST invoice with my order?
A: Yes. A GST invoice is emailed to you at dispatch and a printed copy is included inside the parcel.

Q: Can I add my GSTIN to claim input tax credit?
A: Yes. Enter your GSTIN and registered business name in the "GST Details" field at checkout. The invoice will then be raised as a B2B invoice.

Q: Until when can I add or correct my GSTIN?
A: You can add or correct your GSTIN any time before dispatch by emailing support@troogood.com with your order ID. GSTIN cannot be changed after the invoice is generated.

Q: Can I get an invoice reissued with a different GSTIN after dispatch?
A: No. Once the invoice is filed in our GST return, the GSTIN cannot be changed. Please verify your GSTIN carefully at checkout.

Q: Why is my invoice showing IGST instead of CGST and SGST?
A: We dispatch from Maharashtra. Orders delivered within Maharashtra attract CGST + SGST; orders delivered to any other state attract IGST at the same total rate.

Q: Is GST charged on shipping charges?
A: Yes. Shipping charges are taxed at the same GST rate as the products in your order, and the amount is shown separately on the invoice.

Q: Is GST charged on the COD fee?
A: Yes. The ₹30 COD fee is inclusive of applicable GST and is shown as a separate line item on your invoice.

Q: Will I get a GST credit note for a refund or cancellation?
A: Yes. For full or partial refunds on B2B invoices, we issue a GST credit note against the original GSTIN and email it within 3–5 business days.

Q: Is GST refunded when my order is cancelled or refunded?
A: Yes. Eligible refunds include the full GST amount charged on the refunded items.

Q: Where can I download my GST invoice again?
A: Log in to troogood.com, go to My Orders, and click "Download Invoice." You can also email support@troogood.com with your order ID for a copy.

Q: When will my purchase reflect in my GSTR-2B?
A: B2B invoices are filed in our GSTR-1 for the month of dispatch, so your purchase typically reflects in GSTR-2B in the following month's cycle.

Q: Do you provide GST invoices for bulk and corporate orders?
A: Yes. All bulk, corporate gifting, and distributor orders are billed with a full GST invoice against the buyer's GSTIN. Email support@troogood.com for a proforma invoice.

Q: Do you charge GST on gift cards?
A: No. GST is not charged when you buy a TrooGood gift card. Tax is applied on the actual product purchase when the gift card is redeemed.

Q: Are TDS/TCS provisions applicable to my order?
A: For regular retail and B2B orders, no TDS is deducted by us. For large institutional contracts, applicable TCS under Section 206C(1H) is shown separately on the invoice.
