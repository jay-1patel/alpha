Q: I have chewing problems. Which TrooGood chikki should I buy?
A: For chewing problems, we highly recommend our Dry Fruit Chikki or Kesar Pista Chikki. These variants have a higher nut-to-syrup ratio and use jaggery, making them significantly softer, chewy, and easy on the teeth. They break down easily in the mouth without requiring intense biting force.

Q: Which TrooGood chikki should I buy for my young child?
A: For children, we recommend our Rajgira (Amaranth) Chikki or the Soft Dry Fruit Chikki. The Rajgira chikki is made from puffed amaranth seeds, making it light and easy for young teeth to break. The Dry Fruit Chikki is soft and nutrient-dense. Always break the chikki into small, chewable pieces for children under 7 to prevent choking hazards.

Q: Which TrooGood chikkis are hard and brittle?
A: Our Classic Peanut Chikki and Sesame (Til) Chikki are hard and brittle. We use traditional sugar-boiling techniques to create that authentic, glass-like snap. These variants require strong teeth to bite and chew.

Q: I have braces and sensitive teeth. Can I eat TrooGood chikki?
A: You must avoid our hard and brittle variants (Classic Peanut and Sesame), as they can damage braces and hurt sensitive teeth. However, you can safely enjoy our Soft Dry Fruit Chikki and Rajgira Chikki. These crumble easily with minimal pressure and do not stick aggressively to dental work.

Q: Why is some of your chikki hard while others are soft?
A: The texture depends entirely on the ingredients and the binding agent. Our classic chikkis use pure sugar syrup boiled to a hard-crack stage, which creates a brittle, glass-like texture. Our premium chikkis use organic jaggery and a much higher proportion of nuts and seeds, which results in a softer, chewier, and more crumbly texture.

Q: Can I microwave or heat the hard chikki to make it softer?
A: No, we do not recommend microwaving or heating our hard chikki. Heating sugar-based chikki will melt the sugar syrup, and once it cools down, it will either harden even further or become a sticky, caramelized mess that can cause severe burns. The texture is fixed during our manufacturing process and cannot be safely altered at home.

It requires very little mastication and is often recommended by our customers for older family members who struggle with hard candies or traditional hard peanut chikki.
